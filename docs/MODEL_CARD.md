# Model card (draft)

The model card will be completed after a reproducible training run.

It will document:

- Base model name, immutable revision, and license.
- Adapter method, configuration, seed, tokenizer, and chat/prompt template.
- Training data scope and provenance-manifest hashes.
- Evaluation data, metrics, baselines, and uncertainty.
- Unsupported-claim and memorization tests.
- Intended use: drafting presentation copy from a startup brief.
- Out-of-scope use: financial advice, factual market research without sources,
  or autonomous investment decisions.
- Known limitations and rollback version.
