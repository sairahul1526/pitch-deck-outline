# Data card (draft)

The data card will be completed after the source registry and OCR pilot exist.

It will document:

- Every source, owner, permission reference, license, and allowed purpose.
- Retrieval dates, immutable hashes, and source-version handling.
- PDF extraction and OCR tools, versions, and fallback rules.
- Cleaning, deduplication, language filtering, and quarantine counts.
- PII/secret handling and retention.
- Train/validation/test split policy and company-level leakage controls.
- Known biases, missing industries, and known OCR failure modes.
- Whether raw documents, cleaned text, examples, and model weights may be
  redistributed independently.
